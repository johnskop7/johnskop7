# Hi, I'm Ioannis Litsos 👋

Electrical & Computer Engineering graduate from the National Technical University of Athens (NTUA), with a focus on Machine Learning, Computer Vision, Explainable AI, and Generative Models.

My work includes research on counterfactual explanations for medical images, diffusion models for anomaly localization, reinforcement learning, neural networks, and software engineering projects.

## 🔬 Featured Research

### SPRUCE
**SParse Realistic Uncoupled Counterfactual Explanations in Medical Imaging**

Research framework for generating sparse and realistic counterfactual explanations for medical images using StyleGAN2-ADA, E4E, PTI, PIECE, and classifier-guided latent optimization.

**Presented at SETN 2026**
